### 📋 The 100-Word Speed Read

Working from a tiny desk doesn't mean sacrificing productivity or style. The secret isn't more space—it's smarter choices. 

1. **Start with your monitor:** A single screen on a gas-spring arm frees up 70% of your surface. 
2. **Go wireless:** A compact keyboard and mouse eliminate cable clutter instantly. 
3. **Think vertical:** Use a clamp-on shelf or pegboard to store headphones, notes, and chargers above the desk, not on it. 
4. **Manage power:** Stick a power strip under the desk and use zip ties for cables. 
5. **Upgrade lighting:** Add a small LED bar for warm lighting—it makes any cramped corner feel premium. 

Your small desk isn't a limitation. It's your launchpad.

---

### 🤔 The 3D Mindset Shift

Most people treat their desktop like a flat canvas. On a small desk, that's a mistake. You have cubic feet of empty air surrounding your desk, not just square inches of wood. Wall space, side gaps, and even the dead zone between your desk and your bed are all prime real estate waiting to be utilized.

The key to a great small desk setup is thinking in three dimensions:
* **Up:** Floating shelves, pegboards, and wall-mounted monitors.
* **Sideways:** Rolling carts tucked into gaps beside your desk.
* **Under:** Cable trays and power strips mounted beneath the surface.
